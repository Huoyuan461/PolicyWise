The Service

PolicyWise is a workspace for organizing insurance materials and reviewing policy terms: you paste an insurance link or sales text, or import policy documents, and the app classifies the materials, cross-checks them against the policy text, and surfaces risk points worth your attention in a risk preview and a full report, so you can see what a policy really says before deciding whether to buy it.

The free risk preview is available without registration. Full reports, two-case comparison, and export packages are Pro features, unlocked with an in-app purchase.
