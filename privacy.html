PolicyWise Privacy Policy

Effective date: [To be filled]

This Privacy Policy is provided by Sparkling Wood (Changchun) Software Development Co., Ltd. ("we", "us"), the developer of PolicyWise, and explains what information we collect when you use the PolicyWise app, how we use it, and the rights you have. By using the app, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this policy.

Information We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to provide the service:

1. Account information

  • Information you provide when signing in with Apple or by email, including your Apple ID identifier or email address, and your nickname if you set one. This is used to identify your account and sync your cases and purchase entitlements.

2. Insurance materials you upload

  • Insurance links and sales text you paste, and the PDFs, images, TXT and DOCX files and photos you import. These materials are used only to generate the case classification, risk preview, and full report you request.
  • We do not use your insurance materials for any purpose other than providing the service, and we never sell or rent them to any third party.

3. Purchase information

  • Your in-app purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple. We only verify your purchase status through Apple's receipt validation (for example, whether a report is unlocked or a subscription is active). We do not collect or store your payment card information.

4. Device and usage information

  • Device identifiers, device model, operating system version, and crash and performance logs. Used to keep the service stable, diagnose problems, and improve the experience.

How We Use Information

  • To provide, maintain, and improve the insurance organization and risk analysis service
  • To generate the case classification, risk preview, full report, comparison, and export content you request
  • To verify your purchase entitlements and unlock the corresponding features
  • To respond to your questions and feedback
  • To keep the service secure and prevent abuse and fraud
  • To comply with applicable laws and regulations

Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share necessary information only in the following cases:

  • Service providers: to generate analysis results, the insurance materials you upload are transmitted to the cloud services and compliant large-language-model providers we use. Those providers may only process the information to provide the service, are bound by contract, and may not use it for other purposes or disclose it to third parties.
  • Apple: purchases and subscriptions are completed through the App Store; related transaction information is processed by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.
  • Legal requirements: we may disclose information as required by law or as necessary to protect the safety of users and the public.

We do not share your information with any other third party.

Data Storage and Retention

  • Your case materials and reports are stored on our servers and on your device, and are kept only for as long as necessary to provide the service.
  • You can delete individual cases in the app; the related materials are then removed from our servers.
  • After you delete your account, your account information and all case data are deleted (except information we must retain to meet legal obligations, which is kept only for the period required by law).

Your Rights

  • Access and correction: you can view and edit your account information and case content in the app.
  • Deletion: you can delete individual cases in the app; to delete your account, go to Profile → Delete Account & Local Data → Confirm.
  • Withdraw consent: you can stop using the app and delete your account at any time; withdrawal does not affect processing already carried out on the basis of consent before withdrawal.
  • Contact us to exercise your rights: to access, correct, or delete your information, or for any privacy question, email huoyuan461@qq.com and we will respond within a reasonable time.

Account Deletion

To delete your account, go to Profile → Delete Account & Local Data → Confirm in the app. Your account information, case materials, and local data will be removed. Single-report purchases and the Lifetime membership are tied to your Apple ID and can be restored through the App Store's "Restore Purchases"; auto-renewable subscriptions must be cancelled separately in your system subscription settings, as deleting your account does not cancel them.

Children's Privacy

This app is intended for users aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information, including encryption in transit, access controls, and least-privilege access. Please note that no method of internet transmission or electronic storage is completely secure.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be announced prominently in the app or on this page. The updated policy takes effect on its published effective date. We recommend checking this page periodically.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this policy, contact us at: huoyuan461@qq.com
