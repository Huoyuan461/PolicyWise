About PolicyWise

PolicyWise is a workspace for organizing insurance materials and reviewing policy terms. A single insurance link from an agent often carries 20 or more attachments — too many to open and check one by one. PolicyWise pulls the link, the main text, and every attachment into one case, classifies the materials automatically, and cross-checks them against the policy text to surface the risk points worth your attention, so you can see what a policy really says before deciding whether to buy it.

Key features:

  • Create a case from insurance links or sales text
  • Import PDFs, images, TXT, DOCX files, and photos
  • Automatic classification: main agreements, policy terms, health disclosures, supplemental agreements, and sales notes
  • Free risk preview: top risk points, claim dispute scenarios, and follow-up questions
  • Full report: detailed risk explanations, source references, and export lists
  • Side-by-side comparison of two completed cases
  • Export grouped materials for review
  • Sign in with Apple and email sign-in supported

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create a case?

Tap "New Case" on the home screen and paste the insurance link or sales text you received, or import files directly. The app fetches the attachments from the link, checks them against the main text, and builds your case file.

What materials are supported?

You can paste insurance links and text, import PDFs, images, TXT and DOCX files, and upload photos. Materials are automatically classified into five categories: main agreements, policy terms, health disclosures, supplemental agreements, and sales notes.

What is the difference between the free preview and the full report?

  • Free risk preview: available for every case. It includes the top risk points, scenarios that could lead to claim disputes, and a list of questions to ask the salesperson.
  • Full report: includes detailed explanations of each risk, references to the source terms, and export lists. It is unlocked with a one-time in-app purchase (single report) or an active Pro subscription.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Profile → Delete Account & Local Data → Confirm. Your account information and local data will be removed. Single-report purchases and the Lifetime membership are tied to your Apple ID; deleting your account does not remove them, and you can restore them after reinstalling via "Restore Purchases" on the purchase page. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription — please cancel it separately in your system subscription settings.

How accurate are the risk points in the report?

Reports are generated automatically by comparing the policy text. They are meant to help you spot questions worth asking — they do not guarantee every risk is covered and are not a final interpretation of any clause. If you have questions about a clause, refer to the insurer's official text or consult a licensed insurance professional.
